Belgium is a film set. We're just helping you find your mark.

CineSpot Belgium maps the real locations behind iconic films shot across the country. From the medieval streets of Bruges to the industrial heartland of Wallonia, Belgian cinema has always drawn from the landscapes that surround it. We built this platform to connect cinephiles with the physical spaces that shaped their favorite scenes — and to encourage a new way of experiencing film: on foot, at the source.
