About

CineSpot Belgium maps the real locations behind iconic films shot across the country. From the medieval streets of Bruges to the industrial landscapes of Wallonia, Belgian cinema has always been shaped by the environments that surround it — and in turn, has reshaped how we see those places.

We built this platform to bring cinema and tourism into dialogue — to transform passive viewing into active exploration. CineSpot invites you to stand where a scene unfolded, to compare fiction with reality, and to rediscover places through cinematic imagination.

This is not just film tourism. It is a new way of watching — with your body, your movement, and your presence.
